# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974). Best known in the history of knowledge management and hypertext for his 1945 essay *As We May Think*, in which he proposed the [[Memex]] — a personal device for storing and linking books, records, and notes via associative trails.

## Relevant Ideas

- [[Memex]] (1945) — direct ancestor of hypertext and of the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].
