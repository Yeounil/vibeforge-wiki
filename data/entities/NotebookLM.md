# NotebookLM

Google's AI notebook product. Users upload source documents and ask questions; the LLM answers using [[RAG]] — retrieving relevant chunks from the uploads at query time.

Cited in the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] source as a representative example of the classic RAG approach — answers are re-derived from raw sources on every query, with no persistent compiled wiki layer accumulating over time.
