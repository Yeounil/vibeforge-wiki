# Tolkien Gateway

Community-maintained fan wiki for J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium, with thousands of interlinked pages covering characters, places, events, and languages — built by volunteers over many years. Cited in the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] source as an aspirational scale reference: the kind of richness a personal book-companion wiki could reach if maintenance cost were near zero.
